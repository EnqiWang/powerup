Model & evidence

Historical load profiles are averaged demand proxies. Device shares, essential needs and consent responses are scenario inputs. Stored energy, power limits, 90% charge/discharge efficiencies, constant-load approximation and outage timing are assumed. The preparation scheduler uses priority tiers then shortest backup coverage, not global optimization. No solar or household consumption during preparation is simulated; input headroom excludes background demand. Reserve policies protect against normal-day export, not against using the battery for backup during an outage. Insufficient energy can still interrupt essential service.

The 43-hour replay rounds a 43-hour 2-minute SCE ACROBAT circuit outage in LA County, January 8–10, 2025. It is not a current or typical outage forecast. SCE PSPS rules do not automatically apply to LADWP territory.
