          Caveats
          • The saved replay uses older circuit load values with an inferred conversion of 27.7 kW per unit. The public API now exposes explicit MW fields; those are shown separately in the SCE evidence panel and do not silently change this historical simulation.
          • Serra's load profile is near zero and unusable; the app uses Merlin's shape scaled by customers.
          • Tuna has no SCE line or customer count on record: 500 customers and a coastal band are assumed. Seaboard is drawn for context only.
          • The coast is hand-traced and approximate. Lines are SCE's, simplified to about 10 m.
          • Restoration times in this saved scenario after Oct 17 20:00 are simulated plans, not verified restoration records.
          • SCE does not publish Medical Baseline counts per circuit. The Medical Baseline homes here are demo homes placed by a seeded random draw.
          • All battery homes, resident consent, communications and dispatch actions are simulated. This is not a connected Base Power fleet.
